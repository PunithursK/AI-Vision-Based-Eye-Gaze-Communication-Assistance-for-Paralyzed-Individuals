Project Background & Purpose

Why This Project Was Developed

Severe neurological conditions — including Amyotrophic Lateral Sclerosis (ALS), brainstem stroke, locked-in syndrome, and high-level spinal cord injuries — often strip individuals of all voluntary muscle movement and vocal ability. Yet, their cognitive faculties, thoughts, emotions, and consciousness remain fully intact.

In medical and homecare settings, patients find themselves completely isolated: unable to verbally express pain, call for urgent help, request water or food, or indicate that medication is due. Traditional augmentative communication devices cost thousands of dollars, rely on bulky, invasive sensors, or require motor skills that patients simply do not have.

This system was developed to provide an accessible, low-cost, software-driven lifeline: using an ordinary webcam, computer vision, and machine learning, it translates natural eye gaze dwell and subtle head movements into immediate caregiver dispatches.

Non-Invasive Standard Webcam On-Device ML
Total Motor & Speech Paralysis

Patients with locked-in syndrome or advanced ALS cannot articulate speech or control hand switches, rendering conventional nurse-call buttons useless.

Uncommunicated Vital Needs

Critical requests such as emergency assistance, severe pain, suffocation, hydration, and medication are delayed or missed when caregivers are outside the room.

Cost Barrier of Proprietary Hardware

Commercial eye-trackers frequently cost $5,000–$15,000, creating an insurmountable financial barrier for families and overburdened healthcare facilities.

Preserving Patient Dignity & Autonomy

Enabling touchless communication restores autonomy, diminishes psychological isolation, and empowers patients to interact with their environment independently.

End-to-End Architecture

How the System Works Step by Step

The application operates as a real-time computer vision pipeline, linking MediaPipe FaceMesh, dual-iris geometry, head-pose estimation, adaptive filtering, and caregiver alert dispatchers.

1
Manual Camera Activation

To respect patient privacy, the webcam remains strictly OFF at launch. The caregiver or user clicks "Turn On Camera" in System Controls to initialize the DirectShow video stream at 30 FPS.

2
3D Face & Dual Iris Tracking

MediaPipe FaceLandmarker detects 478 3D landmarks in real time. Subpixel iris localization calculates canthal distances, eye aspect ratios (EAR), and detects micro-blinks to avoid false triggers.

3
3D Head Pose Fusion

Perspective-n-Point (PnP) solves for head yaw, pitch, and roll in real-time. Head movements and dual-iris gaze displacements are adaptively fused to eliminate gaze drift and accommodate natural posture.

4
Trained Gaze Calibration

A 5-point calibration protocol evaluates candidate machine learning models (Ridge, SVR, Random Forest, Affine Standard) with 4-fold cross-validation, mapping eye vectors to true screen coordinates.

5
Adaptive 1-Euro Dwell Selection

Gaze trajectories pass through 1-Euro adaptive low-pass filters. When the gaze cursor dwells inside an option card for the configured dwell duration (default 3.0s), the card confirms with audio feedback.

6
Instant Caregiver Dispatch & Lockout

Upon confirmation, a transactional email is immediately dispatched via Brevo REST API / SMTP to the caregiver, logged in SQLite, and a safety lockout window starts to avoid double-triggering.

Operator & Caregiver Guide

How to Use the Application

Complete step-by-step operating instructions for patients, caregivers, and clinical assistants.

1. Camera Activation (OFF by Default)

The webcam is strictly OFF by default whenever the application starts, preserving privacy and resources:

  • Navigate to the Patient Interface (`/patient`).
  • Click the "System Controls" button in the top navigation bar.
  • Click the red "Turn On Camera" button to open the hardware.
  • Select between System Camera (0) and External USB Camera (1) if multiple devices are attached.
  • Toggle Mirror: ON/OFF depending on whether the patient prefers mirrored interaction.
When camera is OFF, touchless keyboard backup keys (1–4) remain permanently active.
2. Eye Gaze Calibration

Calibration aligns the AI vision model with the patient's individual eye anatomy and seating position:

  • In the System Controls dropdown, click "Calibrate".
  • Five target boxes appear sequentially: Top-Right, Top-Left, Bottom-Right, Bottom-Left, Center.
  • Instruct the patient to focus steadily on each box for 1.5 seconds until it turns green and advances.
  • The system executes 4-fold cross-validation across all 5 points and trains Ridge/Affine models.
  • Calculated metrics (Error MAE, Hit Rate) are saved directly into the SQLite database.
Calibration profiles expire after 24 hours to account for daily lighting and positional changes.
3. Selecting Communication Cards

Four primary high-contrast request cards are arranged in an accessible 2x2 grid:

  • Food (Top-Left): Meal, hunger, or feeding assistance.
  • Water (Top-Right): Hydration, drink, or dry mouth.
  • Medicine (Bottom-Left): Pain medication or scheduled dosage.
  • Help (Bottom-Right): Urgent caregiver assistance or discomfort.

The patient simply fixes their gaze on the desired card. A circular progress ring animates continuously. Reaching 100% triggers confirmation.

Configured dwell time (default: 3.0s) can be customized in Settings (1.5s to 5.0s).
4. Caregiver Notification Workflow

When a selection is completed by gaze or keyboard:

  • An audible audio chime confirms selection to the patient.
  • A high-priority alert email is instantly dispatched via Brevo REST API / SMTP.
  • The email specifies patient name, room number, timestamp, and request category.
  • The screen enters a 60-second safety lockout to reassure the patient while preventing duplicate spam.
  • Caregivers can force-unlock anytime from the interface using the "Unlock" button.
Simulation mode is available for offline testing without consuming email credits.
5. Caregiver Dashboard & Analytics

The dedicated Caregiver Portal (`/caregiver/dashboard`) provides real-time oversight:

  • AI Vision & Calibration Results: Live status, active model, sample count, calibration MAE error, and validation hit rate.
  • Live Hardware Status: Instantaneous camera power, FPS, face detection, and gaze validity.
  • Request History Log: Chronological log of all dispatches with CSV export.
  • Settings: Edit caregiver email, Brevo API key, dwell time, and sensitivity.
Calibration results update automatically every 10 seconds via background polling.
6. Emergency Touchless Keyboard Backup

If camera hardware fails or the patient is fatigued:

  • Press Key 1: Triggers Food request immediately.
  • Press Key 2: Triggers Water request immediately.
  • Press Key 3: Triggers Medicine request immediately.
  • Press Key 4: Triggers Emergency Help immediately.
  • Press Key R: Re-centers baseline gaze resting state.
  • Press Key C: Launches 5-point calibration immediately.
Keyboard inputs execute full email dispatch and database logging identically to gaze dwell.
Core Capabilities

Technical Features & Engineering Highlights

Engineered specifically for clinical reliability, low latency, and maximum patient comfort.

Mandatory Both-Eye Tracking

Simultaneously monitors left and right irises with canthal axis alignment. Automatically engages single-eye fallback if one eye is occluded, maintaining continuous tracking.

3D Head Pose Compensation

Estimates 3D head yaw and pitch via facial mesh reprojection. Fuses eye and head motion to prevent calibration degradation when the patient shifts slightly in bed.

Cross-Validated ML Calibration

Evaluates Ridge, SVR, and Affine regression using 4-fold cross-validation on ground-truth target coordinates. Reports genuine validation error rather than inflated training fit.

1-Euro Adaptive Signal Filtering

Employs dynamic cutoff low-pass filtering: eliminates tremors and micro-saccade jitters during fixation while offering zero-latency tracking during fast eye movements.

Multi-Provider Notification Alerts

Sends instantaneous caregiver emails via Brevo REST API with fallback to standard SMTP. Includes simulated test mode, delivery timing metrics, and full delivery audit trails.

Persistent SQLite Telemetry

Saves all communication events, duration timestamps, and calibration accuracy histories into a local SQLite database with one-click CSV export for clinical documentation.

Operator Guidance

Important Usage Instructions & Troubleshooting Tips

Essential recommendations to achieve optimal gaze accuracy and resolve common operational questions.

Environmental & Positioning Best Practices
  • Optimal Distance: Position the webcam 50–70 cm directly in front of the patient's face at eye level.
  • Lighting Setup: Use diffuse, even ambient lighting. Avoid placing the patient directly in front of bright windows or unshaded backlights.
  • Eyeglasses & Reflections: The subpixel iris detector is compatible with most corrective lenses. If heavy glare occurs, angle the display slightly.
  • Resting Position: Before starting, have the patient look comfortably at the screen centre and click "Re-center".
  • Calibration Recency: Re-run calibration if the patient's bed angle or room lighting changes significantly during the day.
Troubleshooting & Problem Resolution
  • "Camera Sensor Blocked / Camera Off": Click System Controls → "Turn On Camera". Check USB connection and ensure no other app is using the webcam.
  • "Searching for Face": Increase room lighting and center the patient's face in the camera preview window (HUD View).
  • Gaze Cursor Jumps or Drifts: Click "Re-center" (Key 'R'), then run the 5-point calibration again to train a fresh model.
  • Card Dwell Does Not Trigger: Ensure both eyes are detected (check HUD). The patient must maintain fixation inside the card boundary for 3.0 seconds.
  • Emails Not Delivering: Open Settings, verify Brevo API Key or SMTP credentials, and run "Send Test Email".
Medical Disclaimer: This software is an assistive human-computer interface designed to aid communication. It is not an FDA/CE-certified life-support device and must not replace standard bedside nurse call systems, telemetry alarms, or routine physical caregiver check-ins.
